always start from index fund as benchmark.

There exists competition among index funds. Minimizing tracking error is their ultimate goal.

$$
TE = SD(R_{fund} - R_{index})
$$

Reason for tracking error:
- 基金申赎、现金储备
- 指数成分股变更
- 停盘、涨跌停

How to do optimization:
- Leave out some stocks
